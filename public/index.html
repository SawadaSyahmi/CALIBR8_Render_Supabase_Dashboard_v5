PHYSICS INFORMED

Braking capability

How utilisation is calculated

Available deceleration is the smaller of tyre traction and vehicle braking capability. Observed braking removes estimated aerodynamic drag, rolling resistance and the road-grade contribution.

u = observed brake component / available deceleration

Physics label thresholds: normal < 40%, mild < 60%, harsh < 80%, then severe.
